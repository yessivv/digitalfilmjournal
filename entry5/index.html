Shelby Oaks directed by Chris Stuckmann is a Horror/Mystery film starring Camille Sulivan and Sarah Durn


Watching the film Shelby Oaks felt like such a roller coaster of emotions. I was yelling at the screen but also treating it moreso as a video game where I knew the protagonist had to place herself in near death experiences. The film took many dark turns but almost in a comedic scene towards the end. The actual conclusion felt pretty dark if I were to break it down scene by scene although because we were introduced to the demon very early on it kinda lost the scare factor towards the end of the film.
