TUESDAY'S AT ANGELIKA: SICK EDITION

Skins (British TV Series) Gen 1 Directed by Adam Smith, Minkie Spiro, Paul Gay, and Chris Clough


This week since I was sick with a cold I wasn't able to watch a movie at Angelika therefore I made a small entry about my comfort show skins. This show is one I watch when I'm overwhelmed by everything around me. The characters live out such a chaotic lifestyle while being so young themselves. There is an erotic and raunchy aspect to this show that makes it feel very fiction. Although the struggles the characters grow through make me feel peaceful with any worries in my life. This show almost romanticizies struggles which sometimes is the best way to actually deal with my stress.
