Skins (Gen 1) is a british TV series created by Bryan Elsley and Jamie Brittain starring Nicholas Hoult, Kaya Scodelario, Hannah Murray, Dev Patel, April Pearson, and more. Due to being sick this week I wasn't able to go to Angelika therefore I sat in my bed and watched my comfort show. This show is so comedically raunchy and unrelatable but also understands my stress and freakouts over situations that can occur. I am entertained seeing how they walk through life in the most chaotic way. I love the feeling of living vicarously through them. Would recommend this series if you need to feel out your feelings or if you want to watch someone going through it too.
